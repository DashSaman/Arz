# Multi-Edge Spot Research Design

**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**Mode:** deep research, architecture and experiment preregistration only  
**Base:** `research/2026-10-19-edgelab-recovery` at `db79545f5162518251eda73ec9fd3526411f9dc8`

## Intent

Build a source-backed, bilingual research map for spot-only crypto edges without activating live trading, paid services, wallet signing or broad performance optimization. The output must separate data feasibility, economic mechanism, execution constraints and evidence class, then choose only three to five hypotheses for the next test.

## Architecture

The research system is layered: source/data qualification → wallet and market feature specifications → independent opportunity engines → immutable risk boundary → paper/accounting layer. Smart-wallet ranking, copyability, scalpability and meta allocation are specifications, not predictions. Unknown costs, labels, liquidity or legal access remain unknown rather than zero.

## Scope

1. Inspect current repository, branches, PRs, open issues and prior findings.
2. Research current official/primary sources for wallet analytics, swaps, liquidity, quotes, gas, labels and open-source tooling.
3. Produce exact specifications for realized wallet PnL, wallet score, copyability, scalpability and meta-engine inputs.
4. Review spot scalping, trend/rotation, mean reversion, LP, arbitrage, MEV, event-driven and on-chain flow.
5. Rank mechanisms before backtesting using fixed criteria.
6. Select no more than five hypotheses and preregister their data, rules, costs, sample gates, acceptance and rejection criteria.
7. Keep all material documents equivalent in Persian and English.

## Non-goals

- No large backtest grid.
- No wallet discovery presented as profitable without cost-basis reconstruction.
- No transaction signing, approval, deposit, swap, paid API or persistent collector.
- No optimization of thresholds on already observed outcomes.
- No claim that 70–75% win rate is required or sufficient.
- No MEV exploitation, sandwiching or harmful front-running design.

## Evidence and source policy

Prefer protocol documentation, official data/API documentation, original papers and maintained repositories. Every source record includes review date, access/cost status, historical availability, latency, license/redistribution caveat and project implication. Current reachability is not eligibility. Platform labels are features whose historical availability must be proven; they are not ground truth.

## Ranking

Each edge receives fixed 1–5 research scores for economic rationale, data availability, $100 suitability, execution feasibility, cost tolerance, persistence and testability; risk/unknown penalties are separately recorded. Scores rank research priority only and are not probabilities of profit.

## Selected-hypothesis rule

Select three to five mechanisms only if the required point-in-time data can plausibly be obtained without prohibited access. Each candidate must define one shared hypothetical account, exact evidence class, delayed execution, complete costs or explicitly bounded assumptions, no future wallet labels/universe, and predeclared sample/robustness gates. A candidate may be selected for falsification even when expected profitability is unknown.

## Safety

All financial authority remains disabled. Risk controls remain independent of opportunity engines. No research artifact contains private keys, wallet secrets or personal financial identifiers. Public wallet analysis concerns public transaction behavior and avoids identity claims.
