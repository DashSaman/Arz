# Requirements and acceptance criteria

Research date: 2026-10-13. This is a new architectural project. The user has already authorized nonfinancial research, implementation and reversible repository writes; the financial boundary is explicit and separate.

## Objective

Determine whether a transparent spot or unleveraged DeFi strategy has credible net economic merit with experimental capital capped at $100. Produce a reproducible experimental system and a justified GO/PENDING/NO-GO decision. Completing software does not establish profit, operational readiness, legal eligibility or financial authorization.

## Scope and outputs

1. Inspect the real repository, preserve previous work, and establish durable status and decisions.
2. Compare at least the nine named trading engines/modules and a credible additional project, with exact date-stamped GitHub statistics, licenses, version/SHA, activity, actual DEX/spot/AI/backtest support, expenses, security limitations and performance evidence.
3. Compare Jupiter, Raydium, Orca, Meteora, Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Curve and Hyperliquid; compare Solana, Base, Arbitrum and Ethereum without a predetermined winner.
4. Analyze HyperCore, HyperEVM, HLP, legacy/current vault models and spot versus derivatives separately. Analyze unleveraged lending, LP, yield aggregation and market-neutral mechanisms; explain hidden leverage and sources of yield.
5. Evaluate Iranian citizenship/residency restrictions by official interface, API, protocol, RPC, issuer and legal layer. Explicit prohibition is a live blocker; uncertainty remains PENDING. No bypass-based design.
6. Build exact hypothetical growth, cost, withdrawal and loss models. Rates 1%, 2%, 3%, 5%, 10%, 20% monthly are mathematical assumptions, never expected returns. Targets: $100/$300/$500/$1,000 monthly plus an explicit country salary benchmark. Model $200/$500/$1,000/$5,000/$10,000 account goals, loss 10/30/50/100%, cash and buy-and-hold comparisons.
7. Pre-register no more than three simple, long-only candidate strategies before viewing their performance, then execute realistic historical/OOS/walk-forward/stress checks if actual data is available. Preserve provenance, full fills/equity, fees and limitations.
8. Build a CLI-first wallet-free MVP with normalization, strategy, independent risk, simulated execution, accounting, journal and reports. Optional AI is disabled until a separately validated incremental benefit exists. No heavy dashboard needed.
9. Provide equivalent Persian/English research, economics, design, security, installation and results documents, plus structured matrices, reproducible scripts and actual commits.

## Immutable financial boundaries

Initial externally contributed experimental capital <= $100; no external funding path; no borrowing/leverage/martingale; no private key or signing library; no live order endpoint; no paid services. Only separate later authorization could permit a limited real pilot after adequate evidence. A stop is an operational trigger, not a loss guarantee. No Telegram messages are sent: only local report artifacts are authorized here.

## Provisional risk values to test

Initial simulated capital $100, maximum new position/order $25, target position $20, minimum order $5, reserve $10; no more than four fills per UTC day; daily marked-equity loss trigger 3%; account high-water drawdown trigger 10%; adverse modeled slippage cap 50 basis points; network cost per attempted fill cap $0.10; total modeled fee cap $2 per UTC day. These are research settings, not approved live limits. Fee sensitivity also includes more expensive conditions, which may cause rejection or stop rather than hide costs.

## Acceptance gates

Software: deterministic reruns; finite/ordered/complete input checks; cash conservation and no-short/no-negative-cash invariants; lagged fills; cost accounting; idempotent restart; stale/RPC/malformed-data fail-closed; independent risk; live mode always rejected; complete executed test evidence.

Economics: reject unsupported income promises. Qualification requires positive net untouched OOS return, an advantage over matched-exposure and cash benchmarks, tolerable drawdown, robustness to doubled costs, an uncertainty interval supporting positive mean returns, and enough observations/trades. At least 90 prospective paper days and 100 closed paper trades are planning minima, not statistical proof; a low-turnover strategy may require substantially longer. No resetting failed experiments or tuning repeatedly against the same holdout.

Readiness: economic, security, data, operational, access and authorization gates all pass. Missing market regimes, short history, unavailable costs, absence of prospective records or unclear terms keep the result PENDING or NO-GO. A current negative economic recommendation can coexist with useful software research.

## Privacy

The public README displays only “Experimental Trading Capital: Maximum $100”. Other personal balances and private correspondence are excluded. Reports analyze a generic Iran-resident user profile without publishing private identifiers.
