## How to interpret the catalogue

Book entries are publisher descriptions, contents or identity records: **zero full books read**. Two author PDFs have selected methods/examples reviewed, not full original empirical replication. Six biography profiles are initial source-backed career/lesson cards; none includes an independently audited fund history. All remaining requested people have explicit status. The11repositories were inspected through metadata and selected pinned README passages, not installed or security-audited. Sources may overlap; record counts are not independent evidence counts.

Source claims and our proposed Arz applications are separate fields in both languages. A famous person, wealthy manager, large star count or book sales description is not evidence of a tradable100-dollar DEX edge. A legitimate scholarly result using futures/shorting is not a long-only spot backtest.

## Practical findings already acted upon

K01: eight paired stationary-bootstrap diagnostics on existing annual paths, with explicit assumptions and no new market data. K02:56extra-cost fixed-ledger scenarios. K03: three DSR paper examples reproduced; actual Arz DSR/PBO remain unknown because full comparable trial lineage is missing. Existing strategies and risk settings were not retuned.
