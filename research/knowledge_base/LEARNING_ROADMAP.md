# Twelve-week learning roadmap for Arz

This is a proposed learning sequence, not twelve elapsed weeks, a credential, a promise of income or a deadline for profitable trading. A flexible four-to-six hours weekly is a planning assumption. Use free C01–C03 course materials and original small exercises first; Bxx books are optional legal library/publisher supplements, not required purchases. Source definitions and actual access are in SOURCE_REGISTRY.json.

| Week | Learning objective | Free starting material | Practical output and assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
|1|Simple, log and geometric account returns|C01; existing Arz daily-account code|Reconcile a ten-day synthetic account including a loss and flat days. Ending cash must equal compounded returns.|
|2|Sampling, dependence and uncertainty|C02; P09 abstract and T02 documentation|Run K01 on identical synthetic paths and a controlled difference. Explain why bootstrap samples are not new market observations.|
|3|Risk, diversification and common exposure|C01 and C03|Compare cash,20%hold and a simulated rule under matched starting capital. Do not sum independent100-dollar accounts.|
|4|Trend, momentum and reversion mechanisms|P01–P04 abstracts; optional B03|Write two falsifiable economic hypotheses and list why futures or long-short results may not transfer to DEX spot. No optimization yet.|
|5|Researcher and trader decision processes|BIO01–BIO06; optional B14/B15|Separate a biographical fact, an unverified return claim and an original Arz adaptation for each selected profile.|
|6|Failures, incentives and behavioral controls|F01–F04; C03|Build a failure-to-test matrix for duplicate orders, depeg, common liquidity stress and privileged fund access. Mark unimplemented tests explicitly.|
|7|Time-ordered evaluation and data availability|C02; P05/P06 selected methods|Draw label completion and embargo timing. Identify why already-seen October history is not a final holdout.|
|8|Multiple testing and probability scoring|P05/P06; existing Arz forecast scores|Reproduce the DSR paper example, then explain why the actual Arz value remains null. Compare probability scoring with trading profit.|
|9|DEX price formation and execution|T01 official Uniswap guide; optional B06|Map token decimals, exact amount, pool fee, price impact and gas to separate fields. Use no funded wallet or signature.|
|10|Cost and operational feasibility|K02 outputs; C01|Produce fixed-ledger overlays for additional monthly and per-leg costs. Label the difference from a strategy rerun with resized positions.|
|11|Design one bounded experiment|EXPERIMENT_PLAN.json and K04–K06 backlog|Select only a hypothesis with available legal data, freeze rules/baselines/failure criteria and write causality/accounting tests.|
|12|Reproduction and honest decision|Existing diagnostics code and source registry|Rerun with exact input hashes, verify outputs, and deliver REJECT/RESEARCH_ONLY/INSUFFICIENT. No forced financial activation.|

Mathematics sequence: probability and descriptive statistics → conditional inference and time-series dependence → estimation and regularization → optimization under uncertainty. Stochastic calculus, high-frequency control and deep models are optional later specializations, not prerequisites to a safe small-account diagnostic.

Completion means the exercise is reproducible and its limits can be explained. It does not mean every lecture/book was consumed or an edge was discovered.
